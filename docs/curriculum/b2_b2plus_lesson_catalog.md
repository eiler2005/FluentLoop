## 1. Diplomatic Stakeholder Pushback

- Slug: `diplomatic-stakeholder-pushback`
- Topic: Stakeholder communication
- Goal: Push back on a risky plan without sounding defensive.
- Grammar focus: hedging recommendations, modal verbs for suggestions
- Knowledge areas: stakeholders, pushback, register
- Target chunks: push back on, I see one risk, it may be worth
- Mistake risks: too direct disagreement, missing preposition after push back
